Option A · ISOT dataset files

Upload the original Fake.csv and True.csv. Labels are assigned from the file names.

Option B · One labelled CSV

CSV needs one text column (text, article, content, news or title) and one label column (label, class, target or category). Labels should be Fake/Real or 0/1 (0=Fake, 1=Real).

Training workflow

  1. Dataset cleaning and duplicate removal.
  2. Stratified 80/20 train-test split (random seed 42).
  3. TF-IDF with English stop words and unigram/bigram features.
  4. Train Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression and Linear SVM.
  5. Compare accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.
  6. Save the best F1 model and vectorizer for predictions.
